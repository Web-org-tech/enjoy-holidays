## 1. STRATEGY RULES & CONSTRAINTS

1. **Strictly Qualitative Priorities:** In accordance with audit standards, all search volume, keyword demand, and commercial priorities are labeled **Qualitative** (High / Medium / Low) based on local search intent analysis, travel industry booking patterns in Madurai, and real geographical demand. No artificial search volumes or rank numbers are invented.
2. **Offerings Verification ("Offered?" Status):** No route, service, or vehicle tier is asserted as an existing business capability unless explicitly verified in the codebase, database seed records, or live content. Each keyword is tagged with:
   - **`Y` (Confirmed):** Verified in site copy (`ServicesSection.tsx`, `003_seed_data.sql`, `AboutPage`, or `TestimonialsStrip.tsx`).
   - **`Unknown` (Flagged for Owner):** Service or vehicle tier represents common local travel demand but is not explicitly confirmed by the business. Flagged for owner verification in [Section 7](#7-questions-for-the-business-owner).
   - **`N` (Not Offered):** Self-drive rentals, unverified long-distance flights, or unrelated services.
3. **Strict One-Primary-Keyword-Per-Page Rule:** To eliminate internal keyword cannibalization, every indexable page is mapped to exactly **one primary keyword**. No two URLs target the same primary query.
4. **Natural On-Page Usage:** Keywords must be deployed organically within `<title>`, `<h1>`, opening 100 words, semantic `<h2>`/`<h3>` subheadings, image `alt` attributes, and internal contextual anchor text. No keyword stuffing or repetitive city-name jamming.
5. **Anti-Duplication Principle:** No thin, near-duplicate "city doorway pages" are recommended. Dedicated pages are limited to **maximum 6 high-intent destinations/services**, each requiring unique itineraries, route distances, duration, vehicle options, transparent fare mechanisms, custom FAQs, and authentic photos.
6. **NAP Consistency:** Business Name (`PADMA TOURS & TRAVELS`), Physical Address (`No: B19/3 Racecourse Colony, Opp. Old Passport Office, Government Quarters, Madurai - 625002`), and Phone numbers (`+91 98659 87975`, WhatsApp `+91 70101 11256`) remain 100% identical across all on-page metadata, copy, and Schema.org structured data.
